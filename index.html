How it works

Three steps, and the family takes it from there.

1

Capture

Scan a photo or letter, record a voice, or add a video. Bough reads the date and place so you do not type them.

2

Tie it to people

Mark who a memory is about. It appears on each person's branch, so nothing floats unattached.

3

Share within the family

Everyone you invite sees it, adds what they have, and comments. Nothing is public.

Private by design

A private circle. No ads, never sold.

Memories stay inside your family, never public, never sold, never used to sell ads, and never fed to an algorithm. You choose who is in the circle. Sign in with Apple, Google, or your email.

Only your family sees it

A family is the sharing boundary. By default a memory is visible to your family and no one else.

No ads, ever

There is no ad business here. Nothing you keep is turned into a target for someone else.

We never sell your memories

Your photos, letters, and recordings are yours. We do not sell them or use them to build profiles.

You control who is in the circle

Invite the family you want, and remove anyone. The record stays with the people who own it.
