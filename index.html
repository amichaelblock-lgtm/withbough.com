How it works

Add a memory to your tree.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Scan a photo or letter, record a story, or add a video. Bough reads the date and place so you don't have to type them.

  2. 2

    Tie it to people

    Mark who a memory is about. It appears on each person's branch.

  3. 3

    Share within the family

    Everyone you invite can see it, add what they have, and comment.
