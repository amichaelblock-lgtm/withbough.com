Free to start. One plan covers everyone in the family.

Add a parent, a grandparent, a partner, a child, and the tree grows.

Find the photo from three summers ago.

Your brother's photo is buried in the group chat. Add it to Bough and tag the people in it. It stays on their branches, alongside the letters and recordings your family keeps.

Today's photo, on everyone's home screen.

Send a postcard from Sunday lunch and the photo lands on the whole family's iOS home-screen widgets. In the family feed, share memories and catch up in the comments.

Sending postcards is included in the Family plan.

Watch them grow up.

Take one photo of your child each month, or the same tree every season. Bough turns the series into a timelapse.

A note for a birthday years from now.

Seal a note and a few photos for a date you choose. The people you choose know something is waiting, but can't see what's inside until it opens. Vault memories don't appear in the feed or on widgets.

A surprise the whole family makes together.

Everyone except the guest of honor adds cards with photos and notes to one board. On the day, it's revealed to them, and they can see who each card is from.

Your family, and no one else.

Nothing you share is public. There are no ads.
